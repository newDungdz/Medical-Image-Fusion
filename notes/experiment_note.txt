1. Recommended Comparison Structure

A balanced comparison section should contain multiple generations of methods.

Recommended structure:

Classic CNN / early DL
    ↓
Universal benchmark methods
    ↓
Modern Transformer-era methods
    ↓
Architecture-specific competitors
2. Mandatory / Universal Baselines

These methods appear across many papers and model families.

They function as:

universal reference points,
reviewer-recognized benchmarks,
stable comparison anchors.
Highest-priority universal baselines
U2Fusion
IFCNN
CDDFuse
SwinFusion
Interpretation
Method	Typical Role
U2Fusion	universal long-standing benchmark
IFCNN	classic CNN baseline
CDDFuse	modern transformer benchmark
SwinFusion	strong transformer-era benchmark
Practical rule

If experiment size is limited:

prioritize these methods first.
3. Classic Baselines

Purpose:

demonstrate improvement over older deep-learning fusion methods,
provide historical comparison.

Recommended:

IFCNN
DenseFuse
U2Fusion

These are useful even when proposing modern architectures.

4. Modern Transformer-Era Baselines

Purpose:

demonstrate competitiveness against current state-of-the-art methods.

Recommended:

SwinFusion
CDDFuse
DATFuse
TarDAL
Important

Transformer-based methods are now central comparison targets in the field.

Even non-transformer papers frequently compare against them.
